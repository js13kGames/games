Rainicorn sky dash

15-second dash — convert darkness into rainbow, race the clock.

Controls

  • W / Up — jump
  • A / D — move
  • Click — shoot
  • Space — restart

Rules

  • Shoot darkness → rainbow: +10 pts, +1.5 s
  • Rainbow platforms vanish after ~4 s
  • Can't shoot through rainbow platforms
  • On the ground you lose time fast — keep hopping
