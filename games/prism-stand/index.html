🦄 PRISM STAND 🌈

The Great Shadow War: The Void Horde seeks to devour the magic of Lyra, the last Light-Weaver Unicorn. If her light is extinguished, the realm will fall into eternal darkness.

As her ethereal Guardian, you wield the Prism. You must weaponize Lyra's light to fend off swarms of undead, dark wizards, and demonic beasts.

The Void Lords: Every 10 levels, a massive Boss will emerge to challenge you: The Broodmother (🕷️), The Night Terror (🦇), The Abyssal Kraken (🐙), The Lich King (💀), and the Eclipse Dragon (🐉).

RULE 1: Move your mouse to aim the Prism.

RULE 2: Keep enemies inside the rainbow beam to melt them.

RULE 3: The beam's power drops over distance. Keep the Prism close to enemies for maximum damage!
