How to play

This is the tale of two 🦄, John and Jean.

Jean is waiting across the road and running out of patience. But without a pedestrian crossing, John can't get across!

Unicorns can only use 🌈 crossings—help him build one!

For each stripe, look around for matching real-world colors, line them up with your camera, and tap the screen. The closer the match, the more points you score!
