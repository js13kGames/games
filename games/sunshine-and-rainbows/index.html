HOW TO PLAY

Catch the rainbow tiles with your unicorn!
Use S D F H J K L to play.
Press the matching key when the tile reaches the glowing area.

EXCELLENT! = perfect timing
GOOD! = inside the activation area
BAD! = wrong key or missed tile

The tiles and music start slow and get faster as your score increases!

Wrong key or missed tile = lose a heart.
You have 3 hearts.
